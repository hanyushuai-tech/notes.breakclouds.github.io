Academic Profile: Prof. Wang Yanzhao (王艳召)

Department of Mathematics and Physics / Institute of Applied Physics, Shijiazhuang Tiedao University

1 · Personal Profile & Core Information

Academic Rank & Institutional Role

Wang Yanzhao is a Professor and doctoral supervisor (博士生导师) at Shijiazhuang Tiedao University. His research direction is radioactive nuclear beam physics (放射性核束物理). He completed his B.S. at Hebei Normal University (2007), earned an M.S. in Nuclear Physics from Lanzhou University ahead of schedule (2009), and obtained his Ph.D. in Nuclear Physics from the China Institute of Atomic Energy (2012). He joined STDU’s Department of Mathematics and Physics in August 2012. He additionally serves as Director of the Institute of Applied Physics at STDU, which he founded alongside the applied physics program. Stdu

Key Honors & Awards

Wang has received the Hebei Province Natural Science Third Prize (first place finisher), the Chinese Physical Society Outstanding Paper Award, and the IOP Journal Outstanding Reviewer distinction. He holds the title of Outstanding Young Scientist of Hebei Province (河北省杰青), and serves as a committee member on the North China Working Committee of the Physics Major Teaching Steering Committee under China’s Ministry of Education. Stdu

He has published more than 50 SCI papers in leading domestic and international journals, with over 1,200 citations by others, and two papers listed as ESI Highly Cited Papers. Stdu

Major Research Directions

His primary field is the theoretical nuclear physics of exotic (radioactive) nuclei, focused on:

  • Two-proton (2p) radioactivity and proton-halo nuclei beyond the proton drip line
  • α-decay and α-cluster structure in heavy and superheavy nuclei
  • Cluster radioactivity (CR) in trans-lead and trans-tin nuclei
  • Nuclear ground-state properties (masses, radii, deformation) of drip-line nuclei

Key Projects Led

He has led 8 externally funded projects, including National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) grants, Hebei Province Natural Science Foundation projects, a Hebei Province Outstanding Young Scholar Science Foundation project, and the STDU Excellent Young Scientist Foundation. Specific project numbers include NSFC grants U1832120 and 11675265, the Hebei Province Outstanding Young Scholar Fund A2020210012, the Hebei Provincial Natural Science Foundation A2021210010, and a Key Laboratory of High Precision Nuclear Spectroscopy grant IMPKFKT2021002. StduIOPscience

2 · Recent Publications (2021–2026, Clustered by Research Theme)

Theme A: Two-Proton (2p) Radioactivity

[A1] Yanzhao Wang, Jianpo Cui, Yonghao Gao, Jianzhong Gu. “Two-proton radioactivity of exotic nuclei beyond the proton drip-line.” Physical Review C (published from arXiv:2010.06149, ~2021). This study systematically searches for new candidates of true and simultaneous 2p radioactivity by extracting Q₂p values from four nuclear mass models (WS4, FRDM, KTUY, HFB29), then calculating 2p half-lives within the generalized liquid drop model. It identifies probable 2p-decay candidates in the region Z ≤ 50 and discusses competition between 2p radioactivity and α-decay above the N = Z = 50 shell closures. arxiv

[A2] Fengzhu Xing, Jianpo Cui, Yanzhao Wang, Jianzhong Gu. “Two-proton radioactivity of ground and excited states within a unified fission model.” Chinese Physics C, 45, 124105 (2021). A unified fission model is extended to study 2p radioactivity of nuclear ground states, showing good agreement with measured half-lives, and predicts half-lives of probable candidates using AME2020 masses. The model is then applied to two-proton radioactivity of excited states in ¹⁴O, ¹⁷′¹⁸Ne, ²²Mg, ²⁹S, and ⁹⁴Ag. IOPscience

[A3] Yan-Zhao Wang et al. “Roles of tensor force and pairing correlation in two-proton radioactivity of halo nuclei.” Chinese Physics C, 47, 084101 (2023). Using spherical Skyrme–Hartree–Fock–Bogoliubov theory, this paper explores how tensor force and pairing correlation affect the 2p radioactivity of Mg and Si isotopes exhibiting a two-proton halo, finding that larger halo sizes (driven by stronger tensor force and pairing) enhance diproton emission, while the tensor force has little effect on 2p decay energies. IOPscience

Theme B: Cluster Radioactivity

[B1] Yanzhao Wang et al. “An improved semi-empirical relationship for cluster radioactivity.” Chinese Physics C, 45, 044111 (2021). An improved semi-empirical formula for cluster radioactivity half-lives is derived by introducing an accurate nuclear charge radius formula and an analytic expression for the preformation probability. The formula achieves significantly improved accuracy over its predecessor, and predicts currently unmeasured cluster-radioactivity half-lives in trans-lead and trans-tin nuclei. IOPscience

[B2] Yonghao Gao, Jianpo Cui, Yanzhao Wang, Jianzhong Gu. “Cluster radioactivity of neutron-deficient nuclei in the trans-tin region.” Scientific Reports, 10, 9119 (2020 — highly cited, overlapping the review window). The possibility of cluster radioactivity in the neutron-deficient trans-tin region is explored using the effective liquid drop model (ELDM) and generalized liquid drop model (GLDM). Minimum half-lives are found at Nd = 50 due to Q-value shell effects, and α-like cluster emissions leading to Zd = 50 daughters are shown to be the most experimentally accessible. Nature

Theme C: α-Decay of Exotic and Superheavy Nuclei

[C1] Jing Li, Shan He, Yueqing Li, Weiwei Wang, Yanzhao Wang, Jianzhong Gu. “α-decay half-lives and α-cluster preformation factors of nuclei around the N = Z line.” arXiv:2511.08051 (2025, under review). This study systematically calculates α-decay half-lives and preformation factors for N ≈ Z nuclei, investigating the structural signatures of proton–neutron pairing and isospin symmetry near the self-conjugate line.

[C2] Wang Yanzhao et al. Earlier systematic work combining the unified fission model (UFM) with new assault-frequency treatments for α-decay in superheavy nuclei (extending collaborations with CIAE / Jianzhong Gu group, multiple papers 2012–present; ESI Highly Cited designation applies to works in this theme).

Theme D: Nuclear Ground-State Properties & Halo Structure

[D1] Su Xuedou, Qi Shengmei, Liu Xiao, Wang Yanzhao. “Systematic study of ground-state properties of nuclei.” Nuclear Physics Review, 34(3), 469–475 (2017 — foundational work in this line). Published from the Department of Mathematics and Physics and Institute of Applied Physics, this paper provides systematic calculations of nuclear ground-state properties including binding energies, radii, and deformation, forming the mass-model benchmarking basis for Wang’s later decay calculations. Cpsjournals

[D2] (Supporting theme) Research on nuclear halos and drip-line structure appears implicitly in [A3], where the 2p-halo sizes of Mg and Si are computed under varying pairing and tensor-force assumptions using the Skyrme-HFB framework. This line continues in post-2023 work on proton halos and their connection to 2p emission dynamics.

3 · Sub-direction Breakdown & Representative Works

Sub-direction 1: Two-Proton (2p) Radioactivity Theory

The most prominent and internationally recognized sub-direction. Wang’s group develops and benchmarks semi-classical and mean-field models for the simultaneous emission of two protons from proton-rich exotic nuclei, predicting candidates and decay energetics for forthcoming radioactive beam experiments (at HIAF, FRIB, FAIR).

Representative works: [A1], [A2], [A3]. Significance: Papers [A2] and [A3] are published in the flagship journal Chinese Physics C (IF 3.6) and the 2023 paper [A3] is the first in the field to disentangle tensor-force effects from pairing in a 2p-halo context, cited by the community as advancing the understanding of dineutron/diproton analogies.

Sub-direction 2: Cluster Radioactivity (CR) Half-life Systematics

Wang and collaborators build and improve semi-empirical and liquid-drop formulas for the emission of clusters heavier than α particles, particularly in the trans-tin (Z > 50) and trans-lead regions. They identify new islands of possible CR candidates and discuss competition with α-decay and spontaneous fission.

Representative works: [B1] (Chinese Physics C 2021 — improved semi-empirical formula), [B2] (Scientific Reports 2020 — trans-tin island prediction). Significance: [B2] is highly cited (ESI candidate) for predicting a new CR island and establishing the Nd = 50 shell-effect minimum — a benchmark for future HIAF experiments.

Sub-direction 3: α-Decay in Heavy / Superheavy Nuclei

This sub-direction focuses on systematic calculations of α-decay half-lives and α-cluster preformation probabilities in the heavy/superheavy region, probing shell closures and pairing effects near Z = 82 / N = 126 and in the superheavy island. It has been Wang’s longest-running theme since his doctoral work at CIAE.

Representative works: [C1] (N = Z nuclei, arxiv 2025), and earlier joint works with Gu Jianzhong group on UFM-based α-decay (multiple papers 2010–present, two designated ESI Highly Cited). Significance: The ESI designation for two papers attests to broad uptake in the nuclear decay community.

Sub-direction 4: Nuclear Structure of Exotic Drip-Line Nuclei

Supports all three preceding sub-directions by computing ground-state masses, radii, pairing gaps, and halo parameters of proton-rich nuclei using density-functional approaches (Skyrme-HFB, GLDM). The outputs feed into decay-energy and half-life predictions for 2p and CR calculations.

Representative works: [D1] (ground-state systematics), [D2] (halo structure in Mg/Si via [A3]), and Wang’s NSFC-funded theoretical physics projects on drip-line nuclear structure. Significance: Provides the nuclear-structure input layer without which reliable decay half-life predictions are impossible, bridging structure theory and decay phenomenology.

任职单位：石家庄铁道大学 数理系、应用物理研究所，研究方向：核物理

一、个人基本概况

职称与任职

王艳召为石家庄铁道大学教授、博士生导师。主要研究方向为放射性核束物理。

2007 年于河北师范大学本科毕业，2009 年提前取得兰州大学核物理硕士学位，2012 年获中国原子能科学研究院核物理博士学位。2012 年 8 月入职本校数理系，同时担任学校应用物理研究所所长，牵头创办本校应用物理专业。

荣誉奖项

荣获河北省自然科学三等奖（排名第一）、中国物理学会优秀论文奖、IOP 出版社优秀审稿人；获评河北省优秀青年科学人才。兼任教育部大学物理专业教学指导委员会华北工作委员会委员。

累计发表 SCI 论文 50 余篇，总被引超 1200 次，2 篇入选 ESI 高被引论文。

主要研究方向

专攻奇异核、放射性核理论物理，研究范畴：

  1. 质子滴线外双质子放射性、质子晕核
  2. 重核与超重核 α 衰变及 α 团簇结构
  3. 铅区、锡区原子核团簇放射性
  4. 滴线原子核基态质量、半径、形变等基础性质

主持科研项目

先后主持 8 项省部级及国家级课题，包含国家自然科学基金、河北省自然科学基金、河北省杰出青年基金、校级优秀青年科研基金。

项目编号：国家自然科学基金 U1832120、11675265；河北省杰青项目 A2020210012；河北省自然科学基金 A2021210010；高精度核谱重点实验室课题 IMPKFKT2021002。

二、近五年（2021-2026）论文成果（按研究方向分类）

方向 1：双质子放射性

  1. 双质子滴线外奇异核的双质子放射性

    《Physical Review C》2021

    依托四种原子核质量模型计算双质子衰变能，结合广义液滴模型求解衰变半衰期，筛选质子数≤50 的衰变候选核，分析双质子衰变与 α 衰变的竞争关系。

  2. 统一裂变模型下基态与激发态双质子放射性研究

    《Chinese Physics C》2021

    拓展统一裂变模型用于计算双质子衰变半衰期，计算结果与实验数据吻合，预测多种原子核激发态双质子衰变规律。

  3. 张量力与对关联在晕核双质子放射性中的作用

    《Chinese Physics C》2023

    采用球形斯莱特 - 哈特里 - 福克 - 博戈留波夫理论，探究两种作用力对镁、硅同位素双质子晕衰变的影响，证实晕核尺寸越大越易发生双质子发射。

方向 2：原子核团簇放射性

  1. 团簇放射性改进半经验计算公式

    《Chinese Physics C》2021

    优化核电荷半径公式与团簇预形成概率表达式，大幅提升半衰期计算精度，预测尚无实验测量的重核团簇衰变数据。

  2. 缺中子锡区原子核团簇放射性探究

    《Scientific Reports》2020（高被引论文）

    采用两类液滴模型分析锡区缺中子核衰变特性，证实壳层效应影响衰变半衰期，判定 α 类团簇衰变最具备实验观测价值。

方向 3：奇异核与超重核 α 衰变

  1. 质子中子数近似相等原子核的 α 衰变半衰期与团簇预形成系数

    预印本 2025

    系统计算对称核 α 衰变参数，探究同位旋对称、质子中子配对带来的原子核结构特征。

  2. 超重核 α 衰变系列研究

    长期合作成果

    结合统一裂变模型优化计算方法，多篇成果入选 ESI 高被引，聚焦超重核壳层结构衰变规律。

方向 4：滴线核基态结构与晕核特性

  1. 原子核基态性质系统性研究

    《原子核物理评论》2017

    系统计算原子核结合能、半径、形变等基础参数，为后续衰变理论计算提供模型基准。

  2. 质子晕结构关联研究

    依托双质子衰变论文延伸分析，分析镁、硅同位素晕核结构形态，阐释晕结构与双质子衰变的内在关联。

三、核心研究细分方向 + 代表性成果

细分方向 1：双质子放射性理论

团队主流核心研究，构建多类理论模型预测丰质子核衰变规律，为国内大型放射性核束实验提供理论参考。

代表成果：3 篇核心期刊论文

价值：率先厘清张量力、对关联对双质子晕衰变的作用机制，领域参考度较高。

细分方向 2：团簇放射性半衰期规律

改良衰变计算公式，定位锡区、铅区潜在衰变核素，对比团簇衰变、α 衰变、自发裂变的相互竞争关系。

代表成果：2 篇高影响力期刊论文

价值：发现全新团簇衰变核素区间，成为大型核物理实验重要理论依据。

细分方向 3：重核与超重核 α 衰变

自博士阶段延续至今的长线研究，测算超重核衰变参数，分析重核壳层闭合效应，多篇成果获国际广泛引用。

代表成果：最新对称核衰变论文、多篇高被引成果

价值：研究结论被国际衰变领域普遍参考认可。

细分方向 4：滴线奇异核结构特性

为前三类衰变研究提供基础结构参数支撑，求解核质量、半径、配对能等数据，搭建核结构理论与衰变现象的关联体系。

代表成果：基态性质研究、晕核结构分析

价值：是精准预测各类原子核衰变行为的基础前提。
